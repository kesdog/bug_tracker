# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Software teams using a shared bug-operations workspace: developers reporting and resolving issues, senior developers assigning and coordinating work, administrators governing users and projects, and authenticated AI agents processing explicitly reachable tickets.

## Product Purpose

Provide a trustworthy, project-scoped workflow for reporting, assigning, investigating, resolving, and auditing bugs. Success means every authorized user or agent can find the relevant ticket, understand its current state and history, collaborate with the responsible people, and act without crossing project boundaries.

## Brand Personality

Operational, precise, and calm. The interface should feel like a dependable engineering tool that makes state, ownership, and next actions immediately understandable.

## Anti-references

Avoid decorative dashboard theatrics, ambiguous status-by-color interfaces, hidden row actions, dense identity IDs without human-readable names, and generic card-heavy layouts that obscure the work queue.

## Design Principles

1. Make authorization boundaries and remediation explicit.
2. Treat persisted ticket history and server-calculated totals as authoritative.
3. Put usernames, ownership, and next actions ahead of opaque identifiers.
4. Preserve familiar, keyboard-operable product controls and workflows.
5. Keep human and AI-agent contracts consistent unless privacy requires a deliberate difference.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Do not rely on color alone, preserve keyboard and screen-reader operation, support light and dark themes, respect reduced motion, maintain readable zoom and mobile layouts, and use explicit loading, empty, error, and permission states.
